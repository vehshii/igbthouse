About IGBT House

Our business story

VFD repair work often slows down because the model is incomplete, the board revision is unclear or the requested power module has a similar-looking substitute. IGBT House is being built to make that process clearer: identify the drive, record the exact code and source the correct part.

We serve technicians, workshops, factories and maintenance teams looking for IGBT modules, control cards, CPU/main boards, keypads, cooling parts and selected GD/CHF series components.
